# SoloBiz brand brief

Source of truth for every ad made with the Arcads skills. Each claim below is
taken from the live product (`client/templates/landing.html`, `storefront.html`,
`floor.html`, `client/static/manifest.json`). If a claim is not on this page, it
is not approved. Add it here with a source before anyone uses it in an ad.

## One line

SoloBiz is a phone-first app for independent vendors to record sales, expenses
and stock, and to see what they have collected and what customers still owe them.

- **Tagline (site title):** Smart Finance for Independent Vendors
- **Positioning name:** Pocket CFO for Vendors
- **Hero line:** "Track sales and expenses. Know where you stand."
- **Closing line:** "Keep your business records together with SoloBiz."
- **Website:** solobiz.dev (confirm before use; it appears in the landing-page mockup)

## Who buys it

- Independent vendors and small traders who run the business alone or with
  one or two helpers: market stalls, Instagram and WhatsApp sellers, small shops,
  people who sell on credit.
- Home market: Nigeria. Prices are in naira (₦); customers are reached on
  WhatsApp; the community uses Nigerian Pidgin ("Sabi-Plug", "Street Wisdom").
- They run the business from their phone, not a laptop.
- **Why now:** customers who buy on credit and forget, notebooks that get lost,
  money mixed with personal cash, and no idea at month end whether the business
  actually made money.

## The ad must NOT claim

- Prices, "free", free trials or discounts. Pricing is not on the site; ask first.
- User counts, ratings, reviews, testimonials or "trusted by X vendors". None are
  published. Never invent a vendor quote. An actor-led ad is a scripted
  dramatization, not a real customer.
- Bank-grade security, encryption standards, compliance or certifications.
- Loans, credit scores, payments, payouts, tax filing or accounting/audit services.
  SoloBiz records money; it does not move it.
- Native App Store / Play Store apps. It is a web app (PWA) added to the home screen.
- Exact figures as real results. The ₦84,500 / ₦21,200 / ₦63,300 numbers on the
  landing page are labelled SAMPLE DATA. If you show them, keep that label or use
  clearly illustrative numbers.
- That the Floor's recommendations are vetted. The site tells users to confirm the
  seller, price and availability before paying.

## Voice

- Plain, calm and practical. Short sentences. Talk like a fellow trader, not a bank.
- Lead with the vendor's day, not with the software.
- Light Nigerian English and Pidgin are on-brand in hooks and UGC ("Who owe you?",
  "Abeg, write am down"). Keep features and the CTA in clear English.
- No hype words: avoid "revolutionary", "AI-powered", "10x", "guaranteed".
- CTA options: "Create an account", "Get started", "Add SoloBiz to your home screen".

## Visual identity

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand indigo | `#4F46E5` | Primary buttons, highlights, logo badge (theme colour) |
| Indigo hover | `#4338CA` | Pressed / darker accents |
| Ink | `#0F172A` | Headlines |
| Body | `#1E293B` | Body text |
| Muted | `#64748B` | Secondary text |
| Canvas | `#F8FAFC` / `#F7F6F2` | Backgrounds |
| Line | `#E2E8F0` | Borders, cards |
| Money in | `#059669` | Sales, positive cash flow |
| Money out | `#DC2626` | Expenses |

- **Font:** Inter, heavy weights (800) for headlines, tight letter-spacing.
- **Logo:** "SB" white monogram on a rounded blue badge (`references/logo.svg`),
  with the word "SoloBiz" in Inter ExtraBold. Never redraw, recolour or stretch it.
  Add it at the edit with an overlay, not inside a generated image.
- **Look:** clean white cards, thin borders, rounded corners, a phone showing the
  dashboard (Sales collected / Expenses / Net cash flow) and a "Sale saved ·
  Receipt ready to share" toast.
- **Scenes that fit:** Lagos market stalls, a provisions shop counter, a fashion or
  hair business working from a phone, a vendor packing WhatsApp orders, evening
  cash count. Real, warm daylight, not glossy stock-photo offices.
- **Faces:** Black Nigerian vendors, mostly 22–45, women and men, everyday clothes.
